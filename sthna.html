Study One — Sthāna

स्थान · to be housed

A rose has been climbing the same brick wall for a while. The brick holds it, and the rose holds the brick.

Sthāna emerged from looking at that relationship for long enough.

This study is hand-block-printed in cotton, in a single field of greens, with rose medallions held within the brick lattice.

Formats: bedsheet, bedcover, dohar, table linen set.
